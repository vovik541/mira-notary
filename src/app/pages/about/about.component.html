Meet Mira Derkach

Mira Derkach is a California Notary Public and Notary Signing Agent serving clients throughout the greater Sacramento area.

Before focusing on loan signings, Mira spent more than 16 years working in banking and consumer lending, assisting clients with accounts, personal and auto loans, wire transfers, and other financial services.

She has also worked in real estate since 2010, coordinating with buyers, sellers, title and escrow professionals, appraisers, inspectors, and other professionals involved in real estate transactions.

Today, she brings that experience to mobile notarizations and loan signings with a focus on careful document handling, clear communication, punctuality, and respectful service.
