# Copyright, License & Third-Party Asset Audit — miranotary.com

Audit date: 2026-10-29 · Repository state: after commit `d8ba3fe` plus the changes listed in §9.
This is a technical compliance audit, **not a legal opinion**. Nothing below was assumed: every claim
cites a file, package metadata or an upstream source, and anything that could not be verified is
marked **UNVERIFIED / REVIEW REQUIRED**.

Stack: Angular 21 SSR (`@angular/build` application builder) deployed as a Cloudflare Worker with
Workers Assets. Served directory: `dist/mira-notary/browser` (built from `public/`). SSR bundle:
`dist/mira-notary/server` (runs on Cloudflare, not distributed).

distributed to browsers.** The site is technically ready for release from the standpoint of *software and
font licensing*. It is **not** fully cleared on *asset provenance*: several images and icons have no
recorded source or permission trail (see §4). None of these is evidence of infringement; they are
unverified.

No CRITICAL findings. HIGH: none. MEDIUM: asset provenance (portrait/office photos, logo, NNA badges,
partner banner, 13 icons without a recorded source, reviews). Highest-value next steps:

1. Obtain written confirmation from the client (Mira) that she owns / has licensed the logo and photos.
2. Confirm the NNA badge files came from the NNA "Logo Downloads" page of her own profile.
3. Confirm the Idiomatic affiliate terms allow use of the supplied banner.
