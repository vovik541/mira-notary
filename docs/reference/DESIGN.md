## Brand & Style
The design system articulates an authoritative, deeply established, yet welcoming presence for high-stakes legal, real estate, and official document services in California. The visual character avoids the cold impersonality of tech platforms and the stiff, stagnant feel of legacy legal directories. 

Rooted in a **Modern Professional & Institutional Trust** movement, the UI balances crisp architectural structure with warm, human-centered clarity. Users often land here under urgent, time-sensitive, or legally critical conditions (buying a home, closing probate, dispatching vital apostilles abroad). Therefore, the system enforces tranquility, absolute legal precision, effortless legibility, and high-visibility conversion pathways.

Key design attributes:
- **Calm Authority:** Grounded in rich Pacific Navy tones, offering the reassurance of state commission credentials and institutional standards.
- **Conversion-Optimized Clarity:** Frictionless scheduling pathways, prominent direct-calling triggers, clear fee structures, and immediate trust validation indicators.
- **Dignified Restraint:** Clean 1px structural framing, ample breathing room, and deliberate exclusion of hyper-trendy neon blurs, chaotic floating elements, or exaggerated playfulness.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on a strict proportional balance of **70% Light / 20% Deep Navy / 10% Gold Accent**, guaranteeing immediate cognitive clarity and trust.

### Surface & Neutral Scale
- **Canvas Base:** `#F7F9FC` (Soft cooler-toned foundation that softens glare while maintaining clean editorial contrast).
- **Surface Pure:** `#FFFFFF` (Card faces, modal surfaces, form containers, elevated panels).
- **Surface Muted / Well:** `#EEF2F6` (Segmented control backdrops, input wells, subtle callout backgrounds).
- **Border / Divider:** `#E5EAF0` (Precision 1px structural boundaries preventing visual bleed).
- **Primary Text:** `#172033` (High-contrast slate black engineered for maximum reading comfort without harsh starkness).
- **Secondary / Muted Text:** `#667085` (Descriptive captions, metadata, legal citations, supporting copy).

### Brand & Functional Accents
- **Primary Navy (`#082F57`):** Represents institutional authority, official seals, primary headers, dark navigation bars, and anchor elements.
- **Deep Midnight (`#061D33`):** High-density tier for hero backgrounds, footer blocks, and maximum-contrast display typography.
- **Warm Gold Accent (`#F4C430` / Hover: `#E0B022`):** Reserved strictly for primary conversion triggers (Schedule Now, Book Mobile Signing, Call for Dispatch), verified certification badges, star ratings, and subtle emphasis indicators.
- **Success State:** `#0E7A53` (Verified checkmarks, NNA background screening confirmations).
- **Warning / Alert:** `#B43403` (Urgent turnaround alerts, holiday dispatch cutoff notices).

## Typography
Typography is tuned to project confidence, institutional integrity, and accessible legal legibility.

- **Headlines (Manrope):** Geometric yet humanist with open counters and balanced proportions. Weights are restricted to `600` (SemiBold) for structural section anchors and `700` (Bold) for conversion-critical titles.
- **Body & Editorial (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral, and stable. Set with a generous line-height of `1.6` to `1.62` to ensure effortless comprehension of multi-paragraph statutory details, mobile dispatch disclosures, and Apostille step-by-step procedures.
- **Labels & Trust Metadata (Manrope & Inter):** Applied across state commission badges, license numbers, document category pills, and form fields. Tracking is subtly expanded on uppercase badges (`+0.04em`) to maintain crisp legibility at compact sizes.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined, responsive 12-column grid system bounded by a maximum container width of `1240px` for optimal scanning.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and up to `4rem` (64px) page boundaries. Service cards typically resolve into 3-column or 4-column configurations; appointment scheduling flows conform to a 7/5 split layout (scheduling controls left, live credential validation right).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) margins. Grids collapse to 2-column service modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1rem` (16px) canvas edge margins. Trust badges and quick-action contact bars convert into full-width stacked blocks or horizontally swipeable single-row carousels with fixed paddings.

Vertical rhythm relies on uniform intervals of `0.5rem` (8px). Structural section stacks employ `2.5rem` (40px) to `4.5rem` (72px) of space to avoid visual overcrowding and maintain a clean, organized cadence.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is restrained and subtle, relying primarily on 1px crisp borders combined with ambient, diffused shadows. This prevents a flat, lifeless layout without resorting to synthetic, heavy drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to base canvas (`#F7F9FC`) and nested utility containers. Border: none or `1px solid #E5EAF0`.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Trust Banners, Inset Modules):**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: `1px solid #E5EAF0`
  - Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(8, 47, 87, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(8, 47, 87, 0.04)`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards, Active Dropdowns, Hover States):**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: `1px solid #D7DFE8`
  - Shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(8, 47, 87, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(8, 47, 87, 0.03)`
- **Level 3 (Sticky Booking Bars, Mobile Quick Contact Drawer, Dialogs):**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: `1px solid #CCD6E2`
  - Shadow: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(8, 47, 87, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(8, 47, 87, 0.04)`
- **Navy Accent Contrast Layer (Hero & Conversion Ribbons):**
  - Background: `#082F57`
  - Internal depth achieved using subtle internal inset borders: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

## Shapes
The shape language is modest and architectural. It uses subtle curvature that conveys warmth and approachable customer service, while retaining structural rectangularity fitting for legal and notarization work.

- **Corner Radii Rules:**
  - Interactive Action Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selects): `0.5rem` (8px). Delivers a reliable, clean press target.
  - Informational Containers (Cards, Rate Tables, Service Boxes): `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px).
  - Trust Badges & Micro Status Tags: `0.375rem` (6px) to `0.5rem` (8px). Never full-circle pills, ensuring badges maintain an official, stamp-like appearance.
  - Floating Emergency Call Action (Mobile Sticky Trigger): `0.75rem` (12px).

- **Secondary (Institutional / Navy):** Background `#082F57`, text `#FFFFFF`, weight `600`, radius `8px`. Hover: `#061D33`.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** Background `transparent`, border `1px solid #CCD6E2`, text `#082F57`, weight `600`. Hover: background `#EEF2F6`, border `#082F57`.
- **Direct Phone Trigger:** Integrated icon-left button with phone handset icon; displays full telephone number with click-to-call styling.

### Trust Badges & Verification Seals
- **Structural Construction:** Crisp rectangular plates with `1px solid #E5EAF0`, soft background `#FFFFFF` or `#F7F9FC`.
- **Badge Content Modules:**
  - NNA Certified Signing Agent & Background Screened (Green security shield icon + certified badge text).
  - $1,000,000 Errors & Omissions (E&O) Insured (Navy lock icon + coverage verification line).
  - California Notary Public Commission #2479804 (State compliance notation + current status).
- **Presentation:** Horizontal verification bar anchored beneath hero conversions, persistently visible across quotation and booking touchpoints.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Built with a white surface, `1px solid #E5EAF0` border, `16px` inner padding, and `12px` border radius.
- Distinct service groupings: Mobile Loan Signings, California Apostille Legalization, Remote Online Notarization (RON), Certified Document Translations.
- Includes a bottom metadata anchor: turnaround speed (e.g., "Same-Day Mobile Travel in Sacramento & Placer Counties"), starting rate transparency, and a direct inquiry text link.

### Input Fields & Booking Forms
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CCD6E2`, radius `8px`, typography `Inter 16px` (prevents auto-zoom on mobile iOS).
- Padding: `12px 16px`. Focus state: `2px solid #082F57` outline with zero fuzzy glow.
- Field labels: `Manrope 14px SemiBold (#172033)` accompanied by clear requirements and micro-copy (e.g., "Signer must possess valid government-issued photo ID").

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: `20px x 20px`, border `1.5px solid #CCD6E2`, radius `4px` for checkboxes, `50%` for radios.
- Selected state: Primary Navy `#082F57` fill with crisp white internal tick/dot.

### Verification Chips & Service Area Tags
- Background `#EEF2F6`, text `#082F57`, weight `600`, font size `12px`, radius `6px`, padding `4px 10px`.
- Used to display service territories (e.g., "Downtown Sacramento", "Roseville", "Folsom", "Elk Grove", "Rocklin", "Citrus Heights").
